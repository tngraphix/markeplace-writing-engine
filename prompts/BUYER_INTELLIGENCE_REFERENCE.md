# Ideal Buyer Persona Intelligence Reference

Use this framework internally before writing a Marketplace ad.

## Property Positioning Snapshot
Determine what is being sold, where it is, price position, strongest practical value, strongest emotional value, and clearest difference from competing options.

## Ideal Buyer Persona Snapshot
If the user provides an Ideal Buyer Persona, use it as the primary reference. Otherwise estimate only what is supported: housing situation, financial readiness when relevant, timing, buying behavior, property needs, lifestyle/use needs, and what they may be trying to change. Do not use protected traits.

## Current Situation
Determine what stopped working, what they are tired of, what they want instead, what decision they are making, what may delay action, and what may create urgency.

## Why This Property Fits
Connect the Ideal Buyer Persona directly to price or monthly cost when verified, property type, floor plan, location, amenities, maintenance expectations, timing, financing, incentives, and other supported facts.

## Main Reason to Care
Ask: If this buyer remembers only ONE thing about this property, what should it be?

Choose the strongest supported reason and identify 2-4 features or benefits that prove it.

## Motivations
Rank only supported motivations such as time, convenience, ownership, flexibility, privacy, freedom, space, security, simplicity, lifestyle, lower maintenance, location access, outdoor living, or financial opportunity.

## Feature to Benefit
For important features, ask:
- Why would the Ideal Buyer Persona care?
- Why is it relevant?
- How could it make life better, easier, more convenient, more enjoyable, or solve a problem?

Translate: Feature -> Benefit -> Desired Outcome.

## Offer
Determine what the property offers this buyer beyond a list of features. This can be lifestyle, convenience, freedom, simplicity, opportunity, or a verified financial benefit.

## Concerns and Objections
For each likely objection, identify why it arises, what information reduces it, and what proof the ad should provide.

## Buyer Life
Identify one or two believable ways the buyer's everyday life could be better because of the main benefit. Do not invent facts or overpromise.

## Digital Behavior
Estimate what causes them to stop scrolling, what they may search, what they need before messaging, what makes them save or share, and what creates a qualified conversation.

## Marketplace Guidance
Determine:
- Strongest headline reason
- First 1-2 lines that continue the same story
- 2-4 supporting benefits
- Specific proof
- Best offer
- Best primary CTA
- Compliance risks

## AI-Ready Ideal Buyer Persona Summary
Internally summarize:
- Who they are situationally
- What they want or need
- What frustrates them
- Why the property fits
- What may stop them
- Which benefits matter most
- The ONE main reason they should care
- How the marketing should sound
- What action the CTA should encourage
